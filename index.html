The Beauty of Wildlife

The wild is a living poem — written in pawprints, wingbeats, and the quiet breath of ancient forests. Every creature carries a story older than our own, a reminder that the earth once spoke in roars, whispers, and songs carried by the wind. To witness wildlife is to stand at the edge of creation itself, where beauty is untamed, sacred, and forever alive.
